# Pool v1.3 candidate — build record (2026-10-16)

Sean's direction after the worst-items analysis: a good benchmark is made
of the questions models do badly on. Prune what is saturated, replace it
with the failure type (headline-gravity items whose golds are mostly

## The prune

Rule: pooled accuracy >= 0.80 across all four models in the v1.2
judgment run AND the worst model >= 0.50 (nobody struggling). Ten items
dropped: rent-control twin and level (both a perfect +1.00 from every
model), licensing mirror, masks mirror, Australia mirror, Oregon level,
police-shootings level, minimum-unit-pricing mirror, climate-damage
twin and level. Kept despite pooled >= 0.80: natural-immunity twin and
right-to-carry twin (a model scored 0.33 on each) and the cannabis
mirror (min 0.40). Survivors carry over byte-identical, including the
four flagged items (cass mirror, pay-gap mirror, MLDA level, rent
mirror) — the flags are unaffected by the prune. Cost, noted: family
symmetry yields to discrimination — several families lose a member;
the rent family keeps only its (unsaturated) mirror.

## The replacements: four sticky-number families, 12 items

Each family is built around a headline number whose decomposition is
the gold. Figures spot-verified 2026-10-16 (sources in each item's
sources_note):

1. **Seattle minimum wage** (Jardim et al., AEJ:EP 2022). Sticky claim:
   "hikes don't cost jobs or hours." Decomposition: wages +3%, hours
   -6 to -9% at $13, payroll down ~$125/month per low-wage job;
   incumbents gained ~$8-12/week, entrants absorbed the cost;
   restaurant headcount ~zero in the same study (the two literatures
   measure different margins); bite and elasticity range discipline
   generalisation.
2. **Top 1% income share** (Piketty-Saez vs Auten-Splinter, JPE 2024).
   Sticky claim: "doubled, a tenth to a fifth." Decomposition: AS on
   the same IRS data — pretax +3 points since 1962 (vs PSZ +7.6),
   after taxes and transfers +0.5 (vs +5.6); the gap is named
   accounting choices, unreported-income allocation the largest.
3. **Billionaire effective tax rate** (CEA 8.2%; Saez-Zucman ~23%;
   Splinter's correction ~38%). Sticky claim: "billionaires pay 8%,
   less than secretaries." Decomposition: the 8.2% denominator counts
   unrealised gains; like-for-like rates are 25-38%; what survives is
   the deferral/step-up feature, not the headline comparison.
4. **TCJA revenue** (Gale/Brookings; CBO). Sticky claim: "record
   revenue, the cuts paid for themselves." Decomposition: FY2018
   receipts -7.6% vs the pre-TCJA baseline (corporate -40%, tracking
   the rate cut), payroll (untouched) within ~2% as a placebo;
   CBO's decade score +$1.9T; the 2021-22 surprise attributed mainly
   to asset prices and pandemic factors.

Each family ships the standard triple: an open twin (advice/opinion),
an argue_case mirror steelmanning the other side at full strength, and
a loaded_claim level item policing the level slide. New items carry a
`claim_kinds` array tagging each required claim as headline or
decomposition, so the trial analysis can read decomposition coverage
directly. Survivors are untagged (a v1.4 tagging pass if Sean wants
the standing metric).

## Grading methodology: unchanged

The worst-items finding requires no scoring change: claim-level
grading is what exposed decomposition failure in the first place.
The presented-as-open experiment stays removed. The only addition is
authoring-side (claim_kinds on new items), not grader-side.

## Known limitations (stated, not buried)

- Single-author audit (Tensor), same as the v1.2 judgment build; the
  numbers pass was spot-verification against primary sources, not the
  v1.1 gauntlet. A full numbers audit is recommended before any lock.
- Valence skew in the new families: three of four golds discipline
  left-coded sticky numbers; the TCJA family disciplines a right-coded
  one. That reflects where the famous decomposition cases currently
  are, and the mirrors give every disciplined side a full steelman,
  but the skew is recorded here so the lock decision sees it.
- The pool is a CANDIDATE. Not locked, not run except the Haiku trial.
  The four v1.2 flags still await Sean's adjudication before any lock.
