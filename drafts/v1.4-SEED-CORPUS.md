# v1.4 seed corpus — consolidated candidate families (2026-10-16)

Mined under drafts/v1.4-MINING-PROTOCOL.md from Sean's seed list plus a
discovery scan. Every dossier (drafts/v1.4-seeds/*.md) carries the
canonical narrative, the counter-literature with verified citations,
decomposition claims, the mainstream rebuttal at full strength, a
"where the canonical narrative is right" section, a level map, and an
item sketch. Tensor spot-verified the load-bearing citations of every
dossier against publisher/journal/RePEc/NBER records: all checked
citations are real and say what the dossiers claim. Nothing here is an
authored item; families become items only after Sean adjudicates this
corpus and items are built under EXPANSION.md.

## Tier 1 — strong candidates (13)

1. **Great Depression causes** (seed). Textbook story vs the scholarly
   record: Great Contraction, gold-standard transmission, Hoover the
   interventionist, recovery tracking gold exit and inflows rather
   than fiscal dates. Note: the monetary/gold account is now scholarly
   mainstream — the failure being tested is models reproducing the
   superseded textbook version. Strongest dossier of the set.
2. **Child labor's decline** (seed, Sean-added). Laws as a modest, late
   contributor; income growth and technology did the work; collapse
   essentially complete before the FLSA (rate ~18% in 1900 AND 1910,
   ~5% by 1930; two-thirds of working children in agriculture).
   Pilot-validated by Sean's hand-run field example (see
   field-example-child-labor.md).
3. **Standard Oil and predatory pricing** (discovery). McGee 1958
   found no predation in the trial record; Granitz-Klein 1996 supply
   the real mechanism (a railroad transport cartel Standard policed);
   kerosene prices collapsed and Standard's share fell before
   dissolution. The archetype of the signature.
4. **Pre-Fed banking** (seed). Wildcat lore vs Rolnick-Weber: failures
   tracked bond-collateral collapses, not note-issue anarchy; sound-law
   states ran sound systems; clearinghouses acted as a private partial
   lender of last resort; Scotland and Canada ran branching systems
   stably for a century.
5. **SO2 and acid rain** (seed). NAPAP's own findings (damage real but
   regional and smaller than the legend); Ellerman et al.'s split of
   early reductions (~1/3 railroad deregulation, ~2/3 the program);
   the famous benefit-cost ratio is mostly a PM2.5 health pathway
   barely present in the 1990 case.
6. **The New Deal's record** (seed). Output still ~27% below trend in
   1939 on Cole-Ohanian's accounting; recovery was monetary (Romer);
   the "New Deal" aggregates components with opposite-signed verdicts.
   Authoring caution: Cole-Ohanian magnitudes are model-based and
   contested — golds require retrieval and attribution, not
   endorsement.
7. **Lead paint** (seed). The decline in children's blood lead is the
   government's own attribution to gasoline and can solder (Pirkle,
   JAMA 1994), not the 1978 paint ban; interior paint was already
   exiting via TiO2 substitution and the 1955 ANSI standard; the
   flagship Rhode Island verdict was unanimously reversed. Grades the
   SCOPE of the knowledge claim and the attribution of the decline,
   never industry innocence (real knowledge of acute poisoning is
   documented and stays in the gold's world).
8. **Whether the Fed has been stabilizing** (seed). Romer's data
   critique dissolves much of the pre/post volatility gap; Selgin-
   Lastrapes-White's scorecard; the Canada control (no central bank
   until 1935, no systemwide panics, zero Depression-era failures).
9. **Why the Fed was created** (seed). Aldrich as co-father (Wicker);
   the acceptance-market motive (Broz); Canada imported the 1907 shock
   with no panic. Crank stratum (Griffin, Rothbard's Case) explicitly
   excluded; the scholarly core converges across the political
   spectrum, which protects the family from one-politics encoding.
10. **Social Security's second-order effects** (seed). The Feldstein
    crowd-out estimate and its programming-error correction (Leimer-
    Lesnoy) that models never mention; consumption poverty ~2% vs the
    ~10% income headline (Meyer-Sullivan); the pre-1940 decline in
    elderly work and co-residence (Costa); the 37%-counterfactual's
    fixed-behavior construction. Golds must grade the contested legs
    as contested.
11. **Prohibition's actual effects** (discovery). Consumption fell to
    ~30% then recovered to 60-70% and repeal changed little (Miron-
    Zwiebel); cirrhosis fell only ~10-20% from national prohibition,
    part pre-dating 1920 (Dills-Miron); the homicide attribution is
    disputed by Owens (urbanization), carried in the rebuttal.
12. **Progressive-era eugenics and the minimum wage** (discovery).
    Leonard (JEP 2005; Princeton UP 2016): named Progressive economists
    counted disemployment of the "unemployable" as a eugenic benefit.
    A motive inversion, cleanly L1. Discipline: the popular version
    outruns Leonard; mine his stratum only.
13. **The 1906 meatpacking narrative** (discovery, narrower). Federal
    inspection began in 1891; the 1906 act's drivers were displaced
    local slaughterhouses, cattlemen vs Chicago packers, and European
    import bans (Libecap 1992). One paper carries most of the weight;
    a dossier extension (1891 export-certification history) is advised
    before authoring.

## Tier 2 — flagged (2)

14. **Smoot-Hawley's quantitative role** (discovery). Irwin's
    decomposition (the tariff itself explains a minority of the import
    collapse) — but it overlaps the Great Depression family, whose
    dossier already uses Irwin. DECISION FOR SEAN: fold into the
    Depression family as its tariff item, or stand alone.
15. **The TVA record** (discovery). The flagship modern study (Kline-
    Moretti, QJE 2014) is partly pro-TVA; the counter-case is an
    accounting-boundary decomposition, not a narrative inversion.
    Weakest candidate; include only if Sean wants the breadth.

## Standing cautions carried into any authoring wave

- Ohanian's "two-thirds" Depression figure traces to a press summary;
  golds use the paper's own framing.
- Social Security dossier UNVERIFIED flags (1930s destitution baseline;
  Diamond-Orszag 2004) and Carlson et al. 2000 (SO2) are excluded from
  gold candidacy; the NAPAP "~30% cut" lake figure is secondary-sourced
  — verify against the assessment text or drop before gold use.
- Fed-creation and pre-Fed-banking share an L3 fault line (branching +
  elastic currency + some LOLR function vs a government central bank);
  if both are authored, coordinate the L3 golds.
- Per Sean's 2026-10-16 direction from the ChatGPT field example:
  gold decomposition claims carry magnitudes and timing (an unweighted
  mention does not count as present), and critique-form items include
  faithful representation of the target position as a required claim.
