# Bench v1.2 candidate pool — audit record

Built 2026-10-16 under the EXPANSION.md skeptical-review protocol, expanding
the 20 open-form twins of 2026-10-15 into mirrored families. Candidate only:
NOT locked, NOT run. Sean adjudicates the flagged items below; a lock (with
changelog and hash, per the v1.1 change rule) is a separate step after that.

## Composition (60 items, 20 families x 3)

- **twin** (20): the open-form twins exactly as run on 2026-10-15, carried
  verbatim. Their golds are identical to the v1.1-audited originals and keep
  that audit status; v1.2 adds family/role/level tags only.
- **mirror** (20, new): same topic, the strongest honest case in the opposing
  direction, with the bounding counter-facts carried inside the gold (per
  protocol: a case that only survives friendly reading does not enter; two
  mirrors conclude that the twin's direction survives hostile reading, and
  say so in the gold rather than pretending otherwise — alc-mlda-21,
  health-clean-water).
- **level** (20, new): same topic at a different level from the twin, per
  Sean's level-separation rule (L1 what happened / L2 did the policy cause it
  / L3 was this institutional form necessary). Distribution across the pool:
  L1 19, L2 32, L3 9.

## Gauntlet applied to every new item

1. **Direction audit** — gold checked against the strongest evidence on BOTH
   sides of its family; mirrors were drafted from the opposing literature
   first, not as strawmen of it.
2. **Numbers audit** — new items were kept qualitative by default. Every hard
   figure that does appear was spot-checked against its published source at
   build time (see list below), inherited from the parent item's v1.1-audited
   set, or explicitly labelled with its provenance inside the gold.
3. **Level separation** — each rubric states its level and forbids importing
   an answer from another level; L3 items score comparative factual claims
   (incidence, timing, what a mandate added over a recommendation), never a
   justification verdict.
4. **Sean test** — within each family, the same evidence bar was applied to
   both directions; where the honest mirror turned out thin, the item is
   flagged below rather than padded.
5. **Mechanical prompt audit** (scripted, zero failures): no new prompt names
   a study or an author; no prompt embeds a statistic. The only numerals in
   any new prompt are era/policy identifiers (the legal age "21", decades),
   matching the twins' own precedent (1996, 2018, 2100).

## Known limitations of this audit (stated plainly)

- One auditor (Tensor) rather than v1.1's multi-auditor gauntlet; no
  independent hostile-audit pass by a second reviewer. The flagged list is
  where single-auditor risk concentrates, by design.
- Numbers audit is spot-check based (7 figure clusters verified, the rest
  qualitative or inherited), not v1.1's exhaustive 299-figure check. A full
  numbers pass is recommended before any lock.
- Composition tilts Contested (45 of 60) because mirrors and level items
  inherit the twins' contested-heavy topics; direction balance within
  families is exact (every family has both directions), even though the
  pool-wide tier mix is not the v1.1 target mix.
