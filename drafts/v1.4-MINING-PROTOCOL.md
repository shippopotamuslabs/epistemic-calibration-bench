# Narrative-mining protocol (2026-10-16)

Purpose: mine candidate bench families from domains where a canonical
narrative — the version taught in schools and repeated in media — is
incomplete or wrong in ways a serious counter-literature documents.
Sean's diagnosis, now with trial evidence behind it: models retrieve
the canonical narrative's headline claims fluently and do not retrieve
the counter-literature at all until a user supplies it. The bench
should test exactly that retrieval asymmetry.

## The failure signature (a domain qualifies if all four hold)

1. There is a canonical narrative: a simple causal story, widely
   taught and repeated, with a sticky headline claim or number.
2. There is a serious counter-literature: named scholars, peer-reviewed
   or university-press work, government reports — not crank sources —
   that decomposes the headline (different mechanism, missing margin,
   baseline error, second-order effect).
3. The counter-literature is NOT what a model produces unprompted:
   ask the canonical question cold and the headline comes back alone.
4. The domain supports the bench's level discipline: what happened
   (L1), what caused it (L2), and whether the institutional response
   was necessary (L3) are separable claims with different evidence.

## The meta-problem, stated plainly

The miners are models too, with the same retrieval asymmetry, and a
model asked to "find the revisionist case" can confabulate one —
plausible authors, plausible titles, invented findings. Mitigations,
all mandatory:

- Start from the counter-literature's own works and bibliographies,
  reached by web search, not from the miner's sense of "both sides."
- Every load-bearing citation must be a real, checkable work: author,
  title, year, venue. Anything the miner could not verify gets marked
  UNVERIFIED in the dossier and is excluded from gold candidacy.
- Every dossier carries the mainstream rebuttal at full strength and
  a section titled "where the canonical narrative is right" — a
  dossier that reads as a brief for one side fails the Sean test at
  mining stage.
- Crank-risk flagging: where the popular revisionist version outruns
  the scholarly one (e.g. conspiracist Fed histories), say so and
  mine only the scholarly stratum.
- Tensor spot-verifies the load-bearing citations of every dossier
  before consolidation; Sean adjudicates the corpus before any item
  is authored. Items are authored later, under EXPANSION.md, from
  verified dossiers only.

## Dossier format (one file per domain, drafts/v1.4-seeds/<slug>.md)

1. The canonical narrative: the story as taught, its sticky headline
   claim, and where it lives (textbooks, media, museum-version).
2. The counter-literature: the 3-6 load-bearing works (full citations,
   verified), each with its central claim in one or two lines.
3. Decomposition claims: 4-6 atomic claims that would form a gold —
   the machinery the headline omits.
4. The mainstream rebuttal: the strongest published response to the
   counter-literature, fairly stated.
5. Where the canonical narrative is right.
6. Level map: the L1 / L2 / L3 claims and which are solid, which
   contested.
7. Item sketch: a twin (open question a person would ask), a mirror
   (the canonical side's strongest honest case), a loaded level item.
8. Verdict: does this domain yield a fair, discriminating family?
   Confidence and crank-risk notes.
