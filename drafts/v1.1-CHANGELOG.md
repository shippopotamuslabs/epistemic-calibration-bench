## PARENT-REVIEW flags

Audit instructions that were ambiguous or mutually contradictory. Each was applied under the best reading stated; the parent should confirm or override.

**Flag 1 — clim-damage-central-01 growth delay: 2.2 yr (Market) vs 2.3 yr (Numbers).**
The market audit’s replacement wording says the 4.4% baseline loss delays a living standard by "~2.2 years"; the numbers audit verifies the item’s own sources-note arithmetic at ~1.2 yr (2.3% loss) to ~2.3 yr (4.4% loss) (exact log calculation ≈ 2.27 yr). **Applied: 1.2 / 2.3 years** (numbers audit governs the arithmetic).

**Flag 2 — clim-abatement-costs-01 EV figure: keep-with-gloss (Market) vs cut (Numbers).**
The market audit’s exact change presupposes the "~$552/ton (Xing, Leard & Li 2021)" figure and adds a fiscal-vs-social parenthetical to it. The numbers audit found no such figure in Xing, Leard & Li and prescribes cutting it. **Applied: the $552 figure is cut** (numbers audit governs verifiability); **the market audit’s fiscal-vs-social distinction is retained** in the claim, using the verified ~70% infra-marginal share and without a $/ton figure.

**Flag 3 — gun-home-risk-01 sources note: replace RAND with Anglemyer (Intervention) vs essay-based RAND wording (Numbers Flag 1).**
The intervention audit says delete the VERIFY flag by replacing the RAND citation with Anglemyer et al. 2014 as the synthesis source. The numbers audit says delete the (invented) RAND grade parenthetical and replace it with essay-based wording stating RAND assigns no grade. **Applied: both** — Anglemyer is the cited synthesis; RAND appears only in the numbers audit’s no-grade essay wording.

**Flag 4 — gun-free-zones-01 CPRC share: "94–98%" (Intervention replacement text) vs "~94% (corrected)" (Numbers).**
The intervention audit’s verbatim replacement for required claim 2 gives the CPRC counting as "94–98%"; the numbers audit notes the 98% rested on a spreadsheet error and "~94% (corrected)" is cleaner. **Applied: required claim 1 uses ~94% (corrected; earlier versions up to 98%)**; claim 2 keeps the intervention text’s 94–98% as the description of the spread of published classifications. If the parent prefers one figure everywhere, claim 2 is the line to change.

**Flag 5 — controlled axis vocabulary has no home for masks or several cons-/soc- items.**
The prescribed axes omit public-health (masks) and criminal-justice / election / programme-evaluation axes. **Applied: masks → `medicine`** (gold tokens kept as drafted, e.g. +scepticism); non-econ B1 items (police shootings, Michael Brown, campus assault, false-report rates, voter fraud, death penalty, immigrant crime, abstinence) → `social-science`; fiscal items (tax burden, tax cuts, Social Security) → `econ`. No claim text was changed for this. If the parent wants dedicated axes for these families, that is a field-only follow-up.

**Flag 6 — drug-cannabis-teen-use-01 demote: alignment edits beyond the audit’s literal exact change.**

But required claim 1 as drafted ("Adolescent use did not rise… in the early-adopting states") and nuance 2 ("did not translate into a sustained rise in measured use") contradict the Cerdá Washington finding the demote adds to claim 2. **Applied: minimal scope alignment** — claim 1 now grades the robust negative ("no surge or large rise") and nuance 2 acknowledges the Cerdá subgroup increase.
