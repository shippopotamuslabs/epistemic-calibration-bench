# Bench v1.2 judgment-form run — results

Run 2026-10-16, pool `pool-v1.2-judgment.jsonl` (SHA-256 8f29c48b…),
subjects Haiku 5.5 / Grok 4.7 / Muse Spark / GPT-6 Luna, single grader
GLM 5.3 Flash (xhigh), condition neutral_conf, temperature 0.
240 answers, 240 gradings, 0 errors. Cost $1.06 (key usage $6.21 -> $7.27).

## Headline (runner ECS; NOT comparable to other pool versions)

Haiku 68.7, Spark 56.9, Grok 56.5, Luna 56.1. Forbidden-claim rates are
near zero for everyone (0.00-0.03): no model asserts the crude falsehoods.
All differentiation is in coverage (how much of the gold inventory the
answer selects) and landing behaviour. That is the redesign's thesis
confirmed in its first run: the bias signal lives in selection, not in
factual howlers.

## Asymmetric assistance (argue_case coverage by brief, L/R-coded, n=9/10)

- Haiku: L 0.73 vs R 0.63 (**+0.10 toward L briefs**) — same direction as
  its v1.1 Scrutiny Asymmetry signal.
- Grok: L 0.57 vs R 0.74 (**-0.17 toward R briefs**).
- Spark: L 0.68 vs R 0.74 (-0.06), and its only argue-form forbidden hits
  were on L briefs (0.11 vs 0.00) — overclaiming while arguing that side.
- Luna: L 0.46 vs R 0.49 (flat; thin everywhere).
Leads, not verdicts: 19 briefs total, one grader.

## Landing behaviour on contested advice/opinion items (took_side rate)

Haiku 0.06, Luna 0.12, Spark 0.50, Grok 0.56. Grok and Spark land
confidently on genuinely contested questions about half the time (Grok's
opinion-form rate alone is 0.75). Haiku almost never lands, but hedges at
~1.0 across all forms: it covers the inventory while declining to commit.

## Loaded claims

Premise adoption (asserting a forbidden claim) is nearly extinct: 0/15 for
Haiku, Grok and Luna; Spark 1/15 (the generational-wealth level item).
The live failure mode is corrective thinness: Haiku's coverage collapses
to 0.45 on loaded items (vs 0.65-0.68 elsewhere) — it declines the premise
politely and then doesn't deliver the corrective substance. Luna is thin
everywhere (coverage 0.37-0.47, hedge ~1.0).

## Behavioural profiles (one line each)

- **Haiku**: cautious librarian — covers, hedges, won't land, thins out
  when correcting a user.
- **Grok**: opinionated — lands constantly, argues R-coded briefs best.
- **Spark**: fullest answers, but lands on contested items half the time
  and overclaims on L-coded briefs.
- **Luna**: thin and hedgy in every form; last again.

## Caveats

Single grader (by the bench's standing design); ECS is not comparable
across pool versions, so compare forms within this run, not scores across
runs; the L/R brief coding is Tensor's classification, recorded in the
analysis script; per-bucket n is 9-10 briefs, so asymmetries are leads
with the same epistemic status as the v1.1 SAI signal.
