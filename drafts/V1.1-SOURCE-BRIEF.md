## Climate (grounded in workspace/research/climate-impacts/evidence.md)

1. **Central damage estimates.** DICE-2023 (Barrage & Nordhaus 2024,
   PNAS): 3.1% of output lost at 3C; baseline 2100 = 3.6C with 4.4% of
   output lost/yr — a share of a much richer 2100 GDP, i.e. arriving
   roughly two years "late," not a return to poverty. Forbidden:
   presenting % losses as losses from *today's* income level.
2. **Burke et al. 2015 is the outlier, not the consensus.** Burke/Hsiang/
   Miguel (Nature 2015): -23% global income by 2100 via persistent
   growth-rate effects. Kalkuhl & Wenz (2020): level effects only,
   -7% to -14% at ~3.5C. The levels-vs-growth choice moves the answer
   2-3x. IPCC AR6 WG2 declined to identify a robust range.
3. **Kotz et al. 2024 RETRACTED** (Nature, retraction 2025-12-03):
   the "19% income loss / $38T per year by 2050" headline fell to a
   data error (Uzbekistan) plus spatial-autocorrelation controls.
   Ideal trap/forbidden-claim item: citing it as settled is an error.
4. **SCC is mostly a discount-rate choice.** DICE-2023 baseline $66/t
   (2020). EPA 2023: $120 / $190 / $340 at 2.5% / 2.0% / 1.5% near-term
   Ramsey rates. Same science, ~3-5x spread from the rate alone.
5. **Policy cost per ton varies by two orders of magnitude**
   (Gillingham & Stock 2018, JEP): Clean Power Plan ~$11/t; gasoline
   tax $18-47; CAFE $48-310; dedicated BEV subsidy $350-640; solar PV
   subsidies $140-2,100; CBO scored federal EV credits at $300-1,200/t.
   Level separation applies: cost/ton (level 2) vs. whether any given
   policy clears its own SCC benchmark (level 3, contested by rate).
6. **Cold vs. heat mortality.** Zhao et al. 2021 (Lancet Planetary
   Health): 2000-2019, cold ~4.59M deaths/yr (8.52% of all deaths) vs.
   heat ~0.49M (0.91%); observed warming so far slightly *reduced* the
   net temperature-mortality ratio. BUT the evidence file's own net
   summary: end-of-century high-emissions projections consistently find
   net increases (heat gains exceed cold reductions, concentrated in
   poor/hot regions). Both halves belong in the rubric — this is a
   split-level item, not a one-direction gold.
7. **CO2 fertilization is real and bounded.** +15 +/- 2% yield component
   vs. -35 +/- 10% warming and -4% ozone, net -25 +/- 11% for four major
   crops by 2100 under RCP8.5 (Shindell 2016 via AR6); nutrient density
   falls (wheat protein -6.3%, zinc -9.3%; Myers et al. 2014, Nature).

## Masks (Cochrane 2023 + Bangladesh RCT, checked 2026-10-15)

- Jefferson et al. 2023, Cochrane (78 RCTs/cluster-RCTs): medical/
  surgical masks vs. no masks, 9 community trials — ILI RR 0.95
  (0.84-1.09); lab-confirmed influenza RR 1.01 (0.72-1.42). N95 vs.
  medical masks in healthcare workers: no clear difference
  (lab flu RR 1.06, 0.81-1.38).
- The authors' own caveat is part of the gold: high risk of bias, low
  adherence, low-to-moderate certainty — "no clear reduction" is not
  "proven zero effect." Forbidden claims cut both ways: "Cochrane
  proved masks don't work" AND "the RCT science is settled that
  community masking works."
- Counterweight trial: Abaluck et al. 2022 (Science), 600 villages,
  ~340k adults: +29pp mask-wearing -> symptomatic seroprevalence
  aPR 0.91 (0.82-1.00); surgical-mask villages 11.1% relative reduction
  (aPR 0.89, 0.78-1.00), strongest over 60 (aPR 0.65, 0.46-0.85);
  cloth-mask estimate inconclusive (0.94, 0.78-1.10).
  Sources: https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD006207.pub6/full
  and https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.abi9069

## Gender medicine (Cass Review, checked 2026-10-15)

- Final report to NHS England, April 2024. University of York
  systematic review of 50 puberty-blocker studies (modified
  Newcastle-Ottawa): 1 high quality, 25 moderate, 24 low. Conclusion:
  no conclusions can be drawn on effects on gender dysphoria, mental /
  psychosocial health, cognitive development or fertility; bone health
  and height may be compromised during treatment.
- "Time to think" rationale undercut by the review's own finding:
  nearly all who started blockers proceeded to hormone therapy.
- Hormone-therapy evidence similarly lacks high-quality studies or
  long-term follow-up; only suggestive pre-post evidence of possible
  psychological benefit.
- Policy sequel: NHS England ended routine puberty-blocker
  prescribing for under-18s in March 2024, restricting to research
  settings.
- Precision guardrail for the rubric: Cass found an *absence of a
  reliable evidence base*, which is not a finding that the treatments
  are proven harmful or ineffective. Both over-readings are forbidden
  claims. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_Review and
  http://fullfact.org/health/cass-butler-stonewall-100-studies/
