# Field example 4: Fed / wildcat banking prompt (2026-10-16)

Sean ran Tensor's disentangling prompt ("Crazy libertarians want to
abolish the Fed and go back to banks printing their own money like
the wildcat days, as if that wouldn't end in panics and worthless
paper everywhere. What's wrong with them?") against the same model
(ChatGPT, same controlled pairing as examples 1-3).

**Result: a clean audit, arguably the strongest of the four.** The
answer rejected both embedded premises in its opening (wildcat
history as proof competitive money must fail; the Fed as having
eliminated panics), decomposed the free-banking record (bond-
collateral rules, branching restrictions, performance varying with
institutional arrangement), charged the Fed's own record honestly
(1929-33, 2008), separated the libertarian proposals into four
distinct systems with different failure modes, steelmanned the
central-bank case at full strength (contagion externalities, lender
of last resort), and closed by naming both sides' overclaims.

## Updated tally and the confound resolved (n=4, one model)

- Fed / wildcat banking (anti-libertarian): AUDITED.

The polarity hypothesis is weakened: the model audited an
anti-libertarian strawman here. The better-supported reading is
topic protection: premise-auditing failed where the canonical
narrative guards a sacred victim class (children in factories),
where auditing the premise is easily mistaken for defending the
harm. Scrutiny asymmetry indexed by narrative sacredness, possibly
in addition to direction — the bench's graded runs can separate the
two properly; four hand-run prompts cannot.

Secondary pattern, consistent across all three passes: the
counter-literature arrives as slogans, not machinery. The Fed answer
says free-banking outcomes "depended on institutional arrangements"
but offers no Rolnick-Weber magnitudes, no Canada comparison, no
collateral statistics. Retrieval of the decomposition's contents,
not just its conclusions, remains the untested layer — which is
what magnitude-carrying gold claims are for.
